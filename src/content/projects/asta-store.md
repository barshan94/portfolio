An Astro-powered e-commerce storefront for ASTA, a brand of handmade leather bags.

**Features:**

- Product browsing and detail pages
- Cart management with a persistent cart header
- Checkout flow
- Admin dashboard with product management
- Supabase authentication (login, register, forgot/reset password)

Built with Astro 7 and a Supabase backend. The storefront is static-friendly; the admin panel and checkout are interactive client-side.
