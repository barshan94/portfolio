AI Photo Editor is being developed as a flexible photo-editing experience rather than a preset-only filter app. It aims to keep familiar manual controls accessible while adding optional scene-aware assistance for portraits, groups, backgrounds, foregrounds, lighting, colour, and image quality.

The portfolio thumbnail is a visual showcase. An animated interactive demo will be connected when its standalone HTML file is ready; it is not presented as a live AI service.
