Undergraduate thesis: *"Blockchain Internet of Things (bIoT): A review of blockchain healthcare security technologies and its applications and market opportunities."*

This thesis reviews research on how blockchain characteristics—distributed ledgers, decentralization, authentication, auditability, and tamper-evident records—may support security and trust in connected healthcare systems.

**Research areas:**

- Wearable sensors, body sensor networks, and remote patient monitoring
- IoT healthcare architectures, communication networks, protocols, and standards
- Authentication, data integrity, access control, and medical-data privacy
- Healthcare compliance considerations, including HIPAA, without assuming blockchain alone guarantees compliance
- A proposed blockchain-based IoT healthcare architecture and the research gaps identified through literature review

The work is a literature review and proposed architecture, not a claim of a clinically deployed healthcare platform. The thesis document is available in the repository.
