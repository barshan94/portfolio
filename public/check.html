09 — Selected project 01 · flagship

Money Calculator

Evolving personal project

A personal-finance platform built around a ledger-first approach, traceable financial changes and reliable balance calculations.

The engineering problem

  • Financial apps become unreliable when a corrected transaction leaves its old balance effect behind.
  • They break again when a repayment loses the link to the loan it belongs to.

The design principle

  • Balances follow the underlying records — never the other way round.
  • Edits, voids and reversals stay auditable rather than silently rewriting history.
  • Every row is ownership-aware, enforced by PostgreSQL Row Level Security.

Documented capabilities

  • Multi-account tracking
  • Income, expenses, transfers, donations, opening balances
  • Transaction ledger with balanced debit & credit entries
  • Auditable edits, voids and reversals
  • Lending, borrowing, loan repayments, partial settlements, repayment cancellation
  • Loan grouping that preserves individual loans and repayment relationships
  • Tuition students, fees, partial payments, outstanding dues, payment history
  • Deposits, investments and long-term asset tracking
  • Budgets, savings goals, recurring activity, donation targets
  • Reports, cash-flow analysis, net-worth tracking, forecasting
  • Authentication and ownership-aware database operations
